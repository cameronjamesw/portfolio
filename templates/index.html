My Story

A bit more about how I got here.

In June 2026, I moved from England to Massachusetts to start a new chapter of my life with my wife. After spending my career in customer-facing and team-focused roles in the UK, the move gave me the opportunity to step back, reassess where I wanted my career to go, and pursue the technology career I had been working toward for the past few years.

I previously completed a Full Stack Software Development diploma with Code Institute, gaining experience with technologies including Python, Django, React, PostgreSQL and JavaScript. Since moving to the United States, I've been building on that foundation while shifting my focus toward IT support and networking. I've been studying through TryHackMe, developing my understanding of computer systems, networking and cybersecurity, and I'm now preparing for the CompTIA Network+ certification. My goal is to combine this technical knowledge with more than a decade of experience working with people, solving problems and providing customer support.

Changing careers and moving countries at the same time has been a big challenge, but it's also given me a fresh perspective on what I want from my career. I'm motivated by learning how things work, figuring out why something isn't working, and finding a practical solution. I'm looking forward to continuing to build my experience in IT, while bringing the communication, patience, teamwork and problem-solving skills I've developed throughout my previous career with me.
